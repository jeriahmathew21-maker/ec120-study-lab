# EC120 Study Lab

Chapters 1–8 practice: 114 foundation questions, a 40-question timed core mock, a 200-question challenge, and 14 draggable graph exercises.

The separate Original 2025 Midterm section preserves 29 retained questions from the supplied Fall 2025 paper, including option order, tables, original question numbers and five original diagrams. International trade and detailed Chapter 10 questions are omitted; basic Chapter 7 externality questions remain.

This rehearsal runs for 60 minutes, hides solutions until submission, counts unanswered items as incorrect, and shows a score out of 29 with a percentage and full review. Its absolute deadline persists across navigation, reloads and closing. If the page is closed at expiry, submission completes on reopening. Practice grading follows the paper's highlighted choices, checked against the models; it is not verified as an official instructor key. Wording assumptions are explained after submission.

Progress stays in each browser. Export backups to retain or deliberately share answers. The original PDF file and personal results are not uploaded; the requested retained exam questions and diagram excerpts are included in the website.
